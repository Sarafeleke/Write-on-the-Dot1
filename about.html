About Us

About Write on the Dot.

A UK-based virtual assistance and business support service, created to help businesses manage their administrative workloads more efficiently.

Background & Introduction

Bringing Talent and Businesses Together

The idea behind the business is simple: there are talented people around the world with valuable skills and abilities, while many UK businesses need reliable, professional support but don’t necessarily want the expense and responsibilities associated with recruiting additional employees.

Write on the Dot brings these two things together through a managed service that connects businesses with dedicated administrative support.

More Than a Directory or Recruitment Platform

Unlike freelance directories or recruitment platforms, Write on the Dot provides an ongoing managed service, with:

  • Dedicated support
  • UK-based management and oversight
  • An approach tailored to each client’s individual requirements

What We Can Support With

  • General administration
  • Data entry
  • Email & diary management
  • Customer enquiries
  • Research
  • Database administration
  • Wider business support

Flexible support is also available for businesses experiencing increased workloads or needing temporary administrative cover during maternity, paternity, sickness or other staff absences.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Mission Statement

To make reliable, professional administrative support accessible to businesses of all sizes, helping clients save time, manage their workloads and concentrate on growing their businesses.

At the same time, the aim is to create meaningful opportunities for talented people to use their skills, develop professionally and contribute to the success of businesses.

Vision Statement

To become a trusted and recognised provider of managed virtual assistance and business support, building lasting relationships with clients through dependable service, strong management and a commitment to quality.

The longer-term vision is to build a business that connects global talent with UK business opportunities, creating value for both clients and the people delivering the service.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Professionalism

Maintaining high standards in communication, conduct and service delivery, ensuring clients receive professional support throughout their relationship with the business.

Reliability

Building trust by being dependable, meeting agreed expectations and taking responsibility for delivering the support clients need.

Confidentiality

Respecting the privacy of clients and their business information, treating all information with care and discretion.

Efficiency

Helping businesses make better use of their time through organised, practical and effective administrative support.

Integrity & Honesty

Being transparent about the services provided, setting realistic expectations and doing what has been promised.

Client Focus

Understanding that every business is different and taking the time to understand each client’s requirements, priorities and preferred ways of working.

How We Work

Our Approach to Supporting Clients

At Write on the Dot, the focus is on providing more than simply access to a virtual assistant. The service is designed around an ongoing, managed relationship that gives clients confidence that their administrative requirements are being looked after.

01

Understanding Your Business

Taking the time to understand each client’s requirements, workload and the type of support they need.

02

Dedicated Support

Providing dedicated assistance suited to the client’s requirements, helping to build familiarity and continuity.

03

Managed Service

Providing UK-based management and oversight throughout the working relationship, rather than simply introducing a freelancer and leaving the client to manage everything independently.

04

Flexible Solutions

Offering support that can adapt to changing workloads, business demands and temporary staffing requirements.

05

Global Delivery, UK Oversight

Combining a global delivery team with UK-based management to provide flexible and cost-effective administrative support.

06

Building Lasting Relationships

Focusing on communication, accountability and continuous improvement to develop long-term working relationships.

Ultimately, the aim is to make running a business that little bit easier by taking care of the administrative tasks that can otherwise consume valuable time and attention.
